The Price of Freedom

Centuries of struggle etched in time. We remember the milestones that shattered the chains of empire.

1857

The First Cry of Rebellion

The Sepoy Mutiny erupted into the First War of Independence. From the streets of Meerut to the courts of Delhi, the seed of sovereign India was planted in blood and fire.

1919

The Tears of Jallianwala Bagh

Hundreds of innocent men, women, and children were massacred in Amritsar. The tragedy shook the soul of the nation, transforming a movement into an unstoppable demand for Poorna Swaraj (Complete Independence).

1930

The March to the Sea

Armed with nothing but truth and walking sticks, thousands marched 240 miles to Dandi to defy the British salt monopoly, showing the world the unmatched power of non-violent resistance.

1942

The Final Ultimatum

With the historic cry of "Do or Die," the Quit India Movement brought the empire to its knees. Millions flooded the streets, ready to face the gallows rather than live another day in subjugation.

August 15, 1947

Awakening to Life and Freedom

At the stroke of the midnight hour, the tricolor unfurled over a free nation. India awoke from the dark night of colonialism to take her rightful place in the world.
